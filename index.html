Methodology

Understand what the calculator is estimating.

Transport

Distance is multiplied by the selected vehicle or transport-mode emission factor and divided by the number of passengers.

Electricity

Electricity consumption is multiplied by the electricity emission factor after accounting for the selected renewable share.

Food

Daily vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals are estimated using separate starter factors.

Waste

Waste generation is adjusted according to the selected recycling percentage.

Cooking

Cooking time is multiplied by the selected cooking-fuel factor.

Important

The factors in this prototype are illustrative starter values. They should be replaced with documented region-specific factors before scientific or official use.
